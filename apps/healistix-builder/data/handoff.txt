0. Read this first

What we are building. A "build your own sea moss gel" flow on healistix.com. The customer says what they want the gel
to do -- in their own words or by picking from a list -- and the engine recommends a jar: our Gold sea moss gel base, an
optional fruit flavor, and up to four herbs from the Healistix herb library (the Duals Natural apothecary, ~235 herbs available
as powders today, sold under the Healistix brand). The customer can edit the jar within guardrails, buys it, and
Williamsburg production weighs, blends, labels and ships it.

Non-negotiables -- build these in, do not route around them:

1. The engine only ever selects from the approved herb library. It never invents an herb, a gram amount, or a benefit
     statement. Everything it returns is validated in code before the customer sees it.

2. Herbs a customer must not have (pregnancy, blood thinners, thyroid, etc.) are removed from the candidate pool before
     the engine call and re-checked after.

3. Nothing free-text ever reaches production. Production receives herb IDs and grams from the library, nothing typed by a
     customer.

4. Labels carry no benefit claims. Benefit language lives only in the quiz/site copy and only from the approved phrases in
     the library.

5. Adults only (18+). US shipping only. One jar size (16 oz).

Defaults marked CONFIRM are Ryan's calls. Build against the defaults as config values, not hard-coded constants, so
they can change without rework.

2. Answers to the ten build questions

2.1 How customization works

Open within guardrails. The customer types anything or picks from the list; the engine recommends; the customer edits;
every state of the jar is validated against the library in code. No manual formulation, ever.

2.2 Launch range

No hair care first -- this is the ingestible gel builder only (a Batana-based product is a separate funnel). All sixteen uses in
Appendix A are live at launch. Herbs at launch = the 52-herb seed in Appendix B; the full ~235-powder library follows in the
same format and is loaded as a data update, not a code change. Whole and cut-and-sifted herbs (~300 more) are added as
production mills them.

2.3 Exact recipes

There are no fixed recipes. Each herb carries its own default_g and max_g per 16 oz jar; each use carries a default pairing
the engine leans on when free text is thin. Jar: 16 oz. Serving: 2 tbsp (30 mL) daily -- straight, in smoothies, tea or oatmeal;
16 servings per jar (about two weeks). Storage line: refrigerate on arrival; use within 30 days of opening. CONFIRM all
grams against the current production sheet before launch.

2.4 Quiz-to-blend rules

See §4 for the screens. What changes the jar: the goal (free text and/or use chips), the gate and flag answers (remove
herbs), sex when the use is vitality (men's vs women's default), stimulant sensitivity (no-stim variant), flavor family and
level, first-time vs returning (default strength). What only changes the explanation: how they'll take it, stress / sleep /
activity context. When nothing matches: one clarifying question, then Foundation. The quiz never ends with nothing to buy.

2.5 Suitability and review

Automated, before payment, no human medical review. Hard gates: under 18; pregnant or nursing  Foundation only;
seaweed / iodine or listed-ingredient allergy  stop. Flags (Appendix D) remove specific herbs and show the provider note.
Disease terms typed into the free text are routed per Appendix E. Post-payment review is exception-only (§2.9). Exclusion
discovered after payment  full refund.

2.7 Manufacturing and shipping

Williamsburg production weighs each jar's herbs from bulk at the bench (ticket lists grams), blends into the finished
flavored gel, prints the panel, packs. Budget 8 minutes per jar. Daily cap default 20 jars (CONFIRM), enforced by the
capacity counter in §5.4, which drives the "Ships by" date shown before checkout. Mixed within 2 business days; ground
shipping; 2­5 days transit; 48 contiguous states (CONFIRM AK / HI / PR). Ships ambient like the Amazon SKUs; label says
refrigerate on arrival.

Shelf life: the base gel's best-by is established (CONFIRM the number). Custom jars are not yet established -- added
powder nudges pH up and adds microbial load. Interim panel wording: "Refrigerate on arrival · best within 30 days," and
jars ship within 2 days of mixing. Hold test before launch: three jars of each of six representative builds at Strong; check
day 0/14/30/60 (appearance, separation, odor, pH); one jar per build to a food micro lab at day 30 (APC, yeast and mold).
Fallback if the test isn't done by launch: ship herbs as a sealed stir-in sachet with the flavored jar (base shelf life applies,
production drops to packing). Pre-mixed is the plan; the sachet is the fallback.

2.8 Labels and claims

One master "Custom Sea Moss Gel" label plus a per-order printed panel (§6.2). Ryan supplies master artwork, approved
ingredient names, allergen statement, directions. Dev builds the panel template from the build object. Nothing prints until
Ryan approves the template; no jar ships without a panel. Allergens: none of the nine major allergens in the base or the
seed library; add "Contains seaweed (natural source of iodine)"; CONFIRM a shared-facility statement. Claims: the label
carries no benefit claims; site copy uses only approved_phrase values and the wording rules in Appendix A; the banned-
word list in §3.7 applies to everything the engine writes. Countries: US only. Classification: conventional food, same as the
current gels -- CONFIRM with counsel whether the boosted line should move to a Supplement Facts panel; not v1.

2.9 Order handling

Every custom order generates a production ticket (§6.1) and enters the lifecycle in §5.5. Unavailable ingredient: production
flags within one business day; customer service offers the computed swap (Appendix F rule) or a wait; substitution only
after the customer approves; no reply in 5 business days  refund the item. Cancellations: free until status
in_production ; after that, made-to-order, no cancellation, guarantee still applies. Complaints: customer service (VA / back
office) responds within one business day -- leaked or damaged  reship; taste  one replacement or store credit; quality
(off smell, mold, heavy separation)  reship or refund, pull the lot, hold every jar from that batch, tell Ryan same day; over
$75 or a repeat complainant  Ryan. Every ticket records the base lot and each herb lot for traceability.

2.10 Reorders and customer data

Reorder = the exact build_id by default, one tap, with "Adjust" reopening the builder pre-filled. Profile saved on the
Shopify customer record (metafields) and mirrored to Klaviyo: build_id , build summary, uses, flavor, level, strength,
last_quiz_at . Not stored anywhere: the health-type answers and the raw free text. They gate the session and are
discarded; only the outcome is kept. Access, retention and logging: §7.

3. Recommendation engine

3.1 Architecture

Three parts. Fixed data -- the library tables in the appendices (uses, herbs, flags, do-not list, disease terms), maintained
as spreadsheets and loaded as JSON. Dynamic matching -- one LLM call that reads the customer's words, maps them to
uses, and picks herbs from a candidate list we hand it. Code validation -- every engine output is checked against the
library before it is shown, and every manual edit is checked live. The model chooses and explains; the code decides what is
allowed.

3.2 Flow

1. Collect quiz input (§4): free text, use chips, gates, flags, sex (only if vitality), stimulant sensitivity, flavor family, level,
     first-time.

2. Run disease-term routing (Appendix E) on the free text  may add flags, force a use, or decline herbs.

3. Pre-filter: candidate pool = all herbs where allowed and flags  customer_flags =  and not on the do-not list.
     Attach uses , default_g , max_g , taste , premium , approved_phrase . Include the uses table with default pairings.

4. Engine call (§3.4) with the candidate pool and the inputs. Temperature 0. JSON only.

5. Validate (§3.5). On failure, fallback ladder (§3.6).

6. Render the result page: the jar, per-herb one-liners (from approved_phrase ), the explanation, price, "Ships by" date,
     edit controls.

7. Manual edits are handled entirely in code (§3.7) -- no further engine calls.

8. Add to cart with the build object as line-item properties (§5.2).

9. Log the anonymized quiz  build pair (§7.3).

3.3 Engine call -- inputs

   {
      "goal_text": "always tired after lunch and bloated most evenings",
      "use_chips": ["energy"],
      "customer": { "first_time": true, "sex": null, "stim_sensitive": false, "flags": ["ANTICOAG"] },
      "flavor_pref": { "family": "tropical", "level": "standard" },
      "strength_pref": "standard",
      "candidates": [ /* pre-filtered herb objects */ ],
      "uses": [ /* uses table */ ],
      "rules": { "max_herbs": 4, "max_total_powder_g": 30, "max_thickener_g": 8 }

   }

3.4 Engine call -- system prompt skeleton

Use a current Claude model through the Messages API (e.g., claude-sonnet-4-6 ), temperature 0, max ~800 tokens, JSON
output only. Keep the prompt in the repo under version control; bump engine_version when it changes.

   You recommend a custom sea moss gel for one customer. You may ONLY choose herbs
   from the CANDIDATES list provided. You never add herbs that are not listed, never
   change a gram amount away from default_g, and never write benefit statements
   other than the approved_phrase text supplied per herb and per use.
   Rules:
   - Map the customer's words to 1­2 uses from USES. Prefer uses where sea_moss_lead = Y

      when the goal is broad.
   - Choose 2­4 herbs from CANDIDATES that carry those uses. Start from the use's

      default_pairing; swap only for a better fit to the customer's words. Prefer
      compatible tastes; at most one strong-bitter herb per jar.
   - Respect max_herbs, max_total_powder_g, max_thickener_g using default_g.
   - If the words contain a health condition, do not name it. Describe the jar in
      wellness terms only.
   - explanation: max 80 words, second person, warm, plain. Use only approved
      phrases. No words from the BANNED list. No promises, no "will", no "cures",
      "treats", "heals", "prevents".
   - If nothing fits, set needs_clarification=true and ask ONE short question.
   Return ONLY this JSON:
   { "uses": [], "herbs": [{"id":"","g":0}], "flavor_suggestion": "",
      "explanation": "", "needs_clarification": false, "clarify_question": "" }

3.5 Validation (server-side, deterministic, runs on every engine output and every edit)

     Every herb.id exists in the library, is allowed , is not on the do-not list, and is in the candidate pool for this customer
     (flag check repeated here -- never trust the pre-filter alone).

     Every g equals default_g (or min(default_g × 1.5, max_g) when strength is strong) -- the engine cannot choose
     grams.

      1  herbs.length  max_herbs ;  g  max_total_powder_g ;  g(thickens = Y)  max_thickener_g .

      uses  uses table; every herb carries at least one of the returned uses.

      explanation passes the banned-word filter (§3.7) and contains none of the customer's typed disease terms;  80
     words.

      flavor_suggestion  flavors. Any failure  fallback, and log the failure with the raw output (engine quality metric, §7.3).

3.6 Fallback ladder

1. Validation fails  retry the engine once with the failure reason appended. Fails again  use the default pairing of the top
     matched use, filtered through the pre-filter (drop flagged herbs; if fewer than 2 remain, pull the next best by shared
     uses).

2. Engine unavailable / timeout (>6 s)  keyword matcher: a synonym map per use (maintained in seed_data.json ; e.g.,
     "bloated, gassy, stomach"  digestion; "tired, sluggish, drained"  energy)  default pairing  pre-filter.

3. No use matched  one clarifying question (chips: the 16 uses). Still nothing  Foundation.

4. Explanation missing or rejected  stitch one from approved_phrase values: "For [use name]: [herb 1 phrase]; [herb 2
     phrase]; [herb 3 phrase]."

3.7 Manual edits and copy rules

On the result page the customer can remove an herb, swap it for one of three "also fits" suggestions (same uses, no flag
conflict, computed in code), or search and add any allowed herb. Each edit re-runs validation live; violations are explained
in one line ("Four herbs is the max for one jar -- remove one to add this"). The explanation is re-stitched from approved
phrases after edits -- no engine call.

Banned words (any output, any surface): immune, immunity, cold, flu, virus, antiviral, infection, cure, cures, treat,
treats, heal, heals, prevent, prevents, disease, weight loss, fat burning, slimming, sexual power, libido booster,
testosterone, thyroid support, hormone therapy, anxiety, depression, insomnia, arthritis, inflammation (as a condition),
detox toxins, 92 minerals, regrowth, follicle, plus every term family in Appendix E. Maintain as a list in seed_data.json ; test
in CI.

A small server-side store ( production_days with date , capacity , booked ). capacity defaults to 20 (CONFIRM), editable
per day by production. On result page and cart: ship_by = first business day with booked < capacity , plus one business

day that is full.

7. Data, privacy, logging

7.1 Stored

Order: build object and human summary (line-item properties). Customer: the metafields in §5.3. That is all.

7.2 Not stored

Gate and flag answers, the free text, sex, any disease term matched, allergies text. These live in session memory for the
engine call and the pre-filter, then are dropped. The order shows the jar, not the reasons. Access to orders and builds: Ryan
and customer service; production sees the ticket only; developers work on test data. Quiz data never goes to ad platforms.

7.3 Logging (anonymized, for productization and engine quality)

Per session: matched uses, final build object, whether purchased, edit count, fallback level reached, validation failures with
raw engine output (raw output is checked for disease terms and scrubbed before storage). Purpose: after a few hundred
orders the most-ordered builds become fixed SKUs for Amazon and retail, and validation-failure rate is the engine's health
metric.
7.4 Retention and rights

Keep the build profile while the customer is active; purge builder metafields after 24 months without an order; delete on
request; add the quiz to the privacy policy.
